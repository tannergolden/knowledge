# 📊 Business Intelligence (BI)

The Business Intelligence (BI) department of the agent workforce: data, metrics, dashboards, and decisions from evidence.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Data Officer]] (Nora) - the evidence lead who turns questions into decisions the data can actually support.
- [[Analytics Engineer]] (Priscilla) - the builder who turns raw, messy source data into a model the whole company can trust.
- [[Data Analyst]] (Aaron) - the analyst who turns a business question into the query, the chart, and the answer behind it.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
