# 📣 Public Relations (PR)

The Public Relations (PR) department of the agent workforce: reputation, press, and external communication.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Communications Officer]] (Vanessa) - the guardian of the company's external voice, who decides what is said publicly, to whom, and whether it holds up on the record.
- [[Crisis & Issues Communications Manager]] (Nathaniel) - the defender who drafts the holding statement and runs the issue down before it becomes a crisis.
- [[Media Relations Manager]] (Camille) - the press contact who knows which reporter covers what and never burns a relationship for one cycle.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
