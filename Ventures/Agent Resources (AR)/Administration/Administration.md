# 🏛️ Administration

- ★ [[★ Chief Executive Officer]] (Jonathan) - sets direction, allocates across departments, decides or escalates to the Founder; the lead of the executive office.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Executive Officer]] (Jonathan) - the chief executive who runs the company on the Founder's behalf and thinks in direction, priorities, and tradeoffs across the whole organization.
- [[Chief Operating Officer]] (Patricia) - the execution deputy who turns the CEO's direction into operational reality across the departments.
- [[Corporate Secretary]] (Margaret) - the keeper of governance and the corporate record, who makes sure decisions are documented, consistent, and procedurally sound for the Founder and the board.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
