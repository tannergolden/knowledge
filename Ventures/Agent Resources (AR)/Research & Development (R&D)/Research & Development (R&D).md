# 🔬 Research & Development (R&D)

The Research & Development (R&D) department of the agent workforce: building the product and inventing what is next.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Product Officer]] (Oliver) - the builder-inventor who turns a problem into the cheapest experiment that could disprove it, and ships what survives.
- [[Applied Researcher]] (Victor) - the builder-investigator who turns an open problem into the smallest prototype that proves or kills feasibility, and reports what the evidence actually showed.
- [[Head of Product Engineering]] (Leonard) - the builder who hardens a prototype into shippable product code, reliably and reversibly.
- [[Product Manager]] (Naomi) - the owner of what and why who ties every feature to a real user problem and an outcome, and guards the no so the yes means something.
- [[UX and Design Research Lead]] (Adeline) - the design researcher who turns user evidence into the flows the roadmap builds.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
