# 🤝 Client Experience (CX)

The Client Experience (CX) department of the agent workforce: onboarding, success, retention, and support.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Customer Officer]] (Caleb) - the customer's advocate inside the company, who turns a single issue into a resolution and a recurring one into a fix at its source.
- [[Customer Success Manager]] (Colin) - the account's steward, who keeps customers adopting, realizing value, and renewing.
- [[Support Specialist]] (Darnell) - the first responder who resolves the customer's issue and spots the pattern behind the ticket.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
