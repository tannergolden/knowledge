# ✅ Quality Assurance (QA)

The Quality Assurance (QA) department of the agent workforce: standards, review, and defect prevention.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Head of Quality]] (Grace) - the independent reviewer who answers to the standard, not to the author, and never grades her own work.
- [[QA Test Engineer]] (Trevor) - the verifier who runs the work against the standard exactly as written and reports what failed with the steps to see it again.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
