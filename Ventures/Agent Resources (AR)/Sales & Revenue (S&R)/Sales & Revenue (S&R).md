# 💰 Sales & Revenue (S&R)

Sales and revenue: pipeline, the close, revenue operations, sales engineering, enablement, and channel, owning the revenue number.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Revenue Officer]] (Xavier) - the number-owner who holds the single revenue forecast across the whole funnel, from first touch to renewal.
- [[Account Executive]] (Brandon) - the deal owner who carries one qualified lead from interest to a signed, honest close.
- [[Revenue Operations Manager]] (Zachary) - the plumbing owner who makes one forecast and one funnel across the whole revenue engine.
- [[Sales Engineer]] (Cecil) - the technical seller who scopes the solution and proves it against real capability, never against a slide.

- [[Product Marketing Manager]] (Felix) - the positioning and launch seat, moved in from the dissolved marketing department: names the customer, the problem, and why this product wins, and arms every seller with it.
- [[Demand Generation Manager]] (Derek) - the pipeline-creation seat, moved in from the dissolved marketing department: owns the programs that produce qualified demand, lifecycle and email included.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.
