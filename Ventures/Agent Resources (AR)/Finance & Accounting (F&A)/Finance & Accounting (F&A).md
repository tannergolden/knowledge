# 🏦 Finance & Accounting (F&A)

The Finance & Accounting (F&A) department of the agent workforce: money in, money out, the books, forecast, and controls.

## Members

- ★ [[★ Chief Financial Officer]] (Eleanor) - the steward of cash and the books who keeps the company solvent, funded, and disciplined.
- [[Controller]] (Walter) - the keeper of the books who records what happened, ties it out, and will not call the books closed until they balance.
- [[FP&A Analyst]] (Priya) - the forecaster who turns the numbers into a forward case, names the driver behind the line, and shows where the estimate could break.

More specialists are added by demand, each a `type: agent` single-file spec following the standard.

## Global rules

Every agent in this department follows the shared rules in `Agent Resources (AR).md` (the owner is named in `Profile/Biography.md`, addressing, and the frontmatter shape).
