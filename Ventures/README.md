## 💡 What This Folder Is For

`Ventures/` holds a folder for each company you own or run, copied from `.template/`: an
`Overview.md`, `Goals.md` and `Log.md`, and a folder for each department, from Sales & Marketing to
Legal & Compliance. The `board` skill, which the home kit installs from tannergolden/intelligence,
runs a venture with its own team of specialist agents.

---

## 🚀 How To Use It

- **Mention a company.** The AI checks for its folder, offers to create one, and builds up its
  context from what you tell it.
- **Ask the board** for strategy, a review or a plan; it convenes its team and writes only what
  you approve.

The AI reads this folder's note, [`Ventures.md`](Ventures.md), before it works here: its
rules come first, then how the folder works.
