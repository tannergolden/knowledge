# 💰 FINANCE & ACCOUNTING (F&A)

<a name="top"></a>

**Where [Company] keeps its books, plans its money and records who owns it.**

_[Company]'s money only, and never a number that unlocks it._

</div>

---

## 📂 What Goes Here

The money of [Company]: its books and monthly close, its forward model and budget, and its cap table. The `finance-and-accounting` skill's company work lands here: the books, the model, the cap table and company tax; the owner's personal finances never do, since they live in `Profile/Finances.md`. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Financials.md`: what the books show, close by close: the record of every monthly close, the last close in detail, unit economics, debt, and the finance deadlines and open loops.
- `Financial-Model.md`: the forward model built on the closed actuals, with its drivers, scenarios and runway, the operating budget each close is measured against, the variance, and a round's math when one is in play.
- `Cap-Table.md`: who owns [Company], holder by holder, with the option pool, every SAFE and note outstanding, every round and the founding clocks, recorded only from executed documents.
- Working files: the monthly close packages, the operating budget and the model workbooks the skill builds, each named for what it covers and its period (`Close Package YYYY-MM.pdf`). A bank statement, a tax return or a payroll register stays in the accounting system or [Company]'s document store, recorded here only as where it is kept.

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- The owner's own money lives in `Profile/Finances.md`, and the equity documents themselves, drafted for signature, in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/`.
- Financial targets are set in the venture's `Goals.md`, and each metric's definition in `Business Intelligence (BI)/Metrics.md`.
- What a vendor costs under contract, tools included, is in the vendor register in `Operations & Logistics (O&L)/Operations.md`, and one person's pay in the payroll system `Human Resources (HR)/Hierarchy.md` points to.

---

## 📏 Rules

- Work here goes through the write-gate (`Ventures/README.md`, Structure): internal drafts go ahead; moving money, a contract, outside communication, a change to equity and a deletion need the owner's yes.
- `Financial-Model.md` and `Cap-Table.md` belong to the strategic core: the `finance-and-accounting` skill writes them, or the `administration` skill when it convenes the departments. The close, the model and the budget land only once the quality-assurance skill's independent check has cleared them.
- The cap table records only executed documents, and changes only on the owner's yes; a round being modeled stays a draft in `Financial-Model.md`.
- No tax ID number is ever recorded, [Company]'s own included, and no account, card or routing number, password or other credential: only where each is kept.
- Payroll appears here in aggregate: one person's pay, and any holder's or employee's personal identifiers, never do.
