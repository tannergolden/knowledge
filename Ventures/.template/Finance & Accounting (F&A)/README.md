# 💰 [Company] - Finance & Accounting (F&A)

The money of [Company]: its financials, its forward model and its cap table. The `finance-and-accounting` skill's company work lands here: the books, the model, the cap table and company tax; the user's personal finances never do, since they live in `Profile/Finances.md`. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

## What Goes Here

- `Financials.md`: the current-state snapshot of revenue, expenses, burn rate, runway and unit economics.
- `Financial-Model.md`: the forward projections and scenarios the budget, the OKRs and any fundraise rest on.
- `Cap-Table.md`: the ownership record, share classes, option pool and funding rounds, updated on every equity event. Sensitive.
- Working files: statements, budgets, models and tax filings, named for what they cover and their period.

## Rules

- `Financial-Model.md` and `Cap-Table.md` are part of the strategic core: the `finance-and-accounting` skill writes them behind the write-gate, or the `administration` skill when it convenes the departments, and any money moved needs the owner's yes.
- No account number, card number or credential is recorded: only where one is kept.

## What Goes Elsewhere

- The user's own money lives in `Profile/Finances.md`, and the legal side of equity in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/`.
