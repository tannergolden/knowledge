# 🔬 RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT (R&D)

<a name="top"></a>

**Where [Company] plans and builds its products, from research to the roadmap.**

_Promoted ideas become [Company]'s products here._

</div>

---

## 📂 What Goes Here

Product work at [Company]: what is being built and why, what comes next, and the evidence behind it. The company's research and development work lands here: research, ideas, and the building of products, skills and software. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Roadmap.md`: the product vision, the MVP definition, the bets by horizon, the roadmap items each tied to the product decision behind it, the requirements with their acceptance criteria, the release plan, the experiment log, and the research under way.
- Working files: specs, prototypes and design notes, and the research reports and idea records promoted for [Company] from `Inbox/`, each named for what it covers and its date (`Spec YYYY-MM-DD.md`).

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- The product decision itself, what to build, for whom and when, is made in `Administration/Decisions.md`; the roadmap here carries it out.
- What [Company] sells is described in `Sales & Marketing (S&M)/Products.md` and `Services.md` once it ships, and a build lives in its own repository, never here.
- Reference knowledge that is not specific to [Company] goes to `Library/`, and a skill built for [Company] lives outside the vault (`Inbox/README.md`, System Assets).

---

## 📏 Rules

- Work here goes through the write-gate (`Ventures/README.md`, Structure): internal drafts go ahead; moving money, a contract, outside communication, a change to equity and a deletion need the owner's yes.
- When the owner runs the AI workforce, its Research & Development department keeps this folder, led by its ⭐ Director of Research & Development: its records reach the folder through the Chief of Staff, and the Knowledge Vault Manager files them here (`Ventures/README.md`, Who Keeps Each File).
- A finished research report stays held in `Inbox/Research/`, and an idea in `Inbox/Ideas/`, until it is promoted; work on a promoted idea for this venture lands here.
- Each roadmap item names the product decision behind it, and each requirement carries acceptance criteria a verifier can check.
- Research with customers keeps what a participant said apart from what was inferred, and a participant's contact details stay in [Company]'s own systems, never here; no password, key or other secret goes into a spec or a design note.
