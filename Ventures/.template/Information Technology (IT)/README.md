# 🖥️ INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY (IT)

<a name="top"></a>

**Where [Company] maps the systems it runs on, and how they are kept secure.**

_Who holds each [Company] key and where, never the key._

</div>

---

## 📂 What Goes Here

The systems of [Company] and their security: what it runs on, who can reach each system, how each one is backed up and restored, and every change, access review and incident. The company's information technology work lands here: the internal systems and their security. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Stack.md`: the systems register, the architecture, the security program, the runbooks, the change reviews, the access reviews, the incidents, each system choice and why it was made, and the domains.
- Working files: change reviews, incident records, runbooks, the access policy and architecture diagrams, one file each, named for what it covers and its date (`Access Review YYYY-MM-DD.md`).

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- A tool's vendor, cost and renewal are in the vendor register in `Operations & Logistics (O&L)/Operations.md`, and its contract in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/Compliance.md`, where a domain that protects a mark is noted beside the mark.
- The product and its roadmap belong to `Research & Development (R&D)/`, and a build itself to its own repository.
- What the law requires of the data, and any duty to notify after an incident, is for `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/`; a message about an incident is drafted in `Public Relations (PR)/`.

---

## 📏 Rules

- Work here goes through the write-gate (`Ventures/README.md`, Structure): internal drafts go ahead; moving money, a contract, outside communication, a change to equity and a deletion need the owner's yes.
- When the owner runs the AI workforce, its Information Technology department keeps this folder, led by its ⭐ Director of Information Technology: its records reach the folder through the Chief of Staff, and the Knowledge Vault Manager files them here (`Ventures/README.md`, Who Keeps Each File).
- No credential, key, password or recovery code is ever recorded: only where it is kept and who holds it.
- Rotating or revoking a credential, granting, changing or removing a person's access, deleting data and turning off a security control each wait for the owner's yes.
- A security incident is recorded with what happened and what changed, never with the secret involved.
- Each system choice is recorded in `Stack.md` with the alternatives considered, and `Administration/Decisions.md` points to it.
