## 📂 What Goes Here

The systems of [Company], security included: its stack, tools, infrastructure, integrations and domains. The `information-technology` skill's company work lands here: the internal systems and their security. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Stack.md`: every tool, platform, piece of infrastructure and integration used to run and build the venture, and its domains.
- Working files: architecture diagrams, runbooks, access policies and exports.

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- Domain ownership as intellectual property is noted in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/`, and product plans in `Research & Development (R&D)/`.

---

## 📏 Rules

- No credential, key or password is ever recorded: only where one is kept and who holds it.
- A security incident is recorded with what happened and what changed, never with the secret involved.
