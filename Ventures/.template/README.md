## 🚀 Start Here

The folder of [Company], a company the owner runs: its company-wide context at the root, its executive office in `Administration/`, and a folder for each department. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

1. `Overview.md`: what [Company] is, its stage and its status. Read it first.
2. `Goals.md`: current priorities, OKRs and strategic initiatives.
3. `Log.md`: what happened with the venture over time, for recent context.

Then the department files the task needs. Each department's `README.md` says what its folder holds and which skill's work lands there, and `Ventures/README.md`, What Lands Here, maps every department to its skill.

---

## 📏 Rules

Every rule in `Ventures/README.md` holds here. Two of them shape all the work inside this folder:

- Only what the owner says or confirms is written here; nothing is guessed or invented, and [Company]'s context stays apart from every other venture's unless the owner asks to connect them.
- The strategic core (`Administration/`, and in Finance & Accounting `Financial-Model.md` and `Cap-Table.md`) is written only behind the write-gate (`Ventures/README.md`, Structure).
