# 🤝 [Company] - Client Experience (CX)

The customers of [Company]: its client roster, accounts, contacts and their status. Customer work lands here. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

## What Goes Here

- `Clients.md`: the client roster, with each account's contacts and status.
- Working files: client briefs, feedback, support exports and account notes.

## Rules

- Contact details are recorded only as the user provides them, and sensitive values as pointers to where they are kept.
- A client known only through the business stays here; someone in the user's personal life goes to `Memory/Entities/People/`.

## What Goes Elsewhere

- Sales assets and offers live in `Sales & Marketing (S&M)/`, and contracts in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/`.
