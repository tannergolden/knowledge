# 🗓️ [Company] - Cadence

> The rhythm that runs [Company]. Each loop reads the files it names below and writes back to `Goals.md`, `Log.md` and this file; a shorter loop never decides again what a longer loop owns.

---

## The Four Loops

| Loop | Frequency | What it owns | Reads | Writes |
| ---- | --------- | ------------ | ----- | ------ |
| Weekly business review | Weekly | Whether the plan is on track this week: the scorecard's leading numbers, the rocks, last week's actions and the issues | `Business Intelligence (BI)/Metrics.md`, `Goals.md`, recent `Log.md` | A dated `Log.md` entry with the scorecard's status and the new actions, each with an owner and a date, and each rock's status in `Goals.md` |
| Monthly review | Monthly | Money against plan, the month's metric trend, and the OKRs' pace mid-quarter | `Finance & Accounting (F&A)/Financials.md`, `Metrics.md`, `Goals.md`, the month's `Log.md` entries | A dated `Log.md` entry, each key result's current value in `Goals.md`, the monthly package in `Administration/` |
| Quarterly review and planning | Quarterly | The quarter's grades, the stage check, and the next quarter's OKRs and rocks | `Goals.md`, `Administration/Strategy.md`, `Financials.md`, `Metrics.md`, the quarter's monthly reviews | The pre-read in `Administration/`, the grades and the new OKRs in `Goals.md`, a stage change proposed to the owner, a `Log.md` entry |
| Annual planning | Yearly | The thesis, the year's objectives, the split across horizons, and the first quarter's OKRs | `Strategy.md`, `Goals.md`, `Financials.md`, `Overview.md`, the year's quarterly pre-reads | A refreshed `Strategy.md`, the year's objectives and first OKRs in `Goals.md`, a `Log.md` entry |

**Discipline:** the weekly loop reads the leading numbers and the longer loops the lagging ones; every number and every action has one owner; a disagreement is aired and its minority view recorded, and then everyone carries out the call; the chair states a view last.

**By stage:** the operating stage `Overview.md` records sets the loops. <!-- found runs a weekly build-measure-learn check in place of a full review, with a monthly board; scale runs all four loops, the board moving from monthly toward quarterly; maintain runs a quarterly board and quarterly OKRs, and keeps Overview.md, Goals.md and Log.md current. -->
