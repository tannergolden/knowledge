# 📦 OPERATIONS & LOGISTICS (O&L)

<a name="top"></a>

**Where [Company] writes down how it runs: processes, SOPs, vendors and routines.**

_[Company]'s processes as they run now, every change dated._

</div>

---

## 📂 What Goes Here

How [Company] runs day to day: its processes and SOPs, the programs that carry a change to done, its vendors and what each one costs, and its recurring tasks. The company's operations and logistics work lands here: processes, programs and vendors. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Operations.md`: the operating model and its constraint, the processes and their SOPs, the program plans, the vendor register with each vendor's cost, renewal, fallback and scorecard, tools included, the recurring tasks, the operations KPIs and the known bottlenecks.
- Working files: SOPs and runbooks, program plans, vendor decisions and scorecards, and drafts of an RFP, a scope of work or a purchase order, each named for what it covers and its date (`Program Plan YYYY-MM-DD.md`).

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- Customer onboarding and offboarding are kept in `Customer Experience (CX)/Clients.md`.
- A vendor's contract terms are in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/`, the tools themselves in `Information Technology (IT)/Stack.md`, and quality checks in `Quality Assurance (QA)/`.
- Each KPI's definition is in `Business Intelligence (BI)/Metrics.md`, and the budget line a purchase draws on in `Finance & Accounting (F&A)/Financial-Model.md`.

---

## 📏 Rules

- Work here goes through the write-gate (`Ventures/README.md`, Structure): internal drafts go ahead; moving money, a contract, outside communication, a change to equity and a deletion need the owner's yes.
- When the owner runs the AI workforce, its Operations & Logistics department keeps this folder, led by its ⭐ Director of Operations: its records reach the folder through the Chief of Staff, and the Knowledge Vault Manager files them here (`Ventures/README.md`, Who Keeps Each File).
- A process is recorded as it runs now; a change to it is dated, and an outdated version is superseded rather than silently rewritten.
- A vendor's bank details, a credential, a door or alarm code, or anyone's personal data is never recorded: only where the document that holds it is kept.
