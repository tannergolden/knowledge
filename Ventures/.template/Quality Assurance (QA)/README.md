# ✅ [Company] - Quality Assurance (QA)

Quality at [Company]: its standards, review processes, checklists and quality metrics. Quality work lands here. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

## What Goes Here

- `Standards.md`: the quality standards, review processes, checklists and the metrics that track them.
- Working files: review records, audit results and checklists in use.

## Rules

- A standard says how it is checked, and a review records its date and its outcome.

## What Goes Elsewhere

- The processes being checked live in `Operations & Logistics (O&L)/`, and the product in `Research & Development (R&D)/`.
