# ✅ QUALITY ASSURANCE (QA)

<a name="top"></a>

**Where [Company] sets its quality bar and checks consequential work against it.**

_Every [Company] standard says how it is checked._

</div>

---

## 📂 What Goes Here

Quality at [Company]: the bar each kind of work is judged against, and the record of every check made against it. The company's quality assurance work lands here: the independent verification of consequential work before it goes out. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Standards.md`: the definitions of done, the review process, the test plans, the verification record, the defect log with each root cause, the quality metrics, and the audits and certifications.
- Working files: test plans, test results, verification reports, defect reports and audit reports, each named for what it covers and its date (`Verification Report YYYY-MM-DD.md`).

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- The processes being checked live in `Operations & Logistics (O&L)/`, and the product's requirements and their acceptance criteria in `Research & Development (R&D)/Roadmap.md`.
- Each quality metric's definition is in `Business Intelligence (BI)/Metrics.md`, and a certification's legal or regulatory duty on the calendar in `Legal & Compliance (L&C)/Compliance.md`.
- A verdict also travels with the work it judged, kept by the department that made the work.

---

## 📏 Rules

- Work here goes through the write-gate (`Ventures/README.md`, Structure): internal drafts go ahead; moving money, a contract, outside communication, a change to equity and a deletion need the owner's yes.
- When the owner runs the AI workforce, its Quality Assurance department keeps this folder, led by its ⭐ Director of Quality Assurance: its records reach the folder through the Chief of Staff, and the Knowledge Vault Manager files them here (`Ventures/README.md`, Who Keeps Each File).
- A standard says how it is checked, and a verdict comes from someone who did not make the work, with its date, its evidence and its outcome.
- A verdict clears the work's quality, never its authority: an act the work enables still waits for the owner's yes.
- Personal data met in a document or a sample under test is cited by where it lives, never copied into a report.
