# ✅ QUALITY ASSURANCE (QA)

<a name="top"></a>

**Where [Company] sets its quality bar and checks consequential work against it.**

_Every [Company] standard says how it is checked._

</div>

---

## 📂 What Goes Here

Quality at [Company]: its standards, review processes, checklists and quality metrics. The `quality-assurance` skill's company work lands here: the independent verification of consequential work before it goes out. Read this README before changing anything in this folder; its rules add to `Ventures/[Company]/README.md`, `Ventures/README.md` and the root `README.md`, and never loosen them.

- `Standards.md`: the quality standards, review processes, checklists and the metrics that track them.
- Working files: review records, audit results and checklists in use.

---

## ↪️ What Goes Elsewhere

- The processes being checked live in `Operations & Logistics (O&L)/`, and the product in `Research & Development (R&D)/`.

---

## 📏 Rules

- A standard says how it is checked, and a review records its date and its outcome.
