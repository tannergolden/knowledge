# 📍 Places

The registry of places of **personal significance** to the user: home, hometown, and the locations they frequent or that carry meaning. One file per place, holding what it is, where it is, and why it matters to the user.

## Scope

- **Personal significance only.** A place earns a file because the user has a relationship to it - they live there, grew up there, return to it, or hold it meaningful - not because it is well known. General facts about a city or landmark are `Library/` reference, not Entities.
- **Personal, not venture.** A business location tied only to one of the user's ventures is `Ventures/` context; a place becomes a personal Entities entry when its significance is personal.
- **Earns a file when real or recurring.** A place named once in passing stays a `Log.md` line until it proves meaningful or recurring.

## How entries are made

Copy `Memory/.template/Place.md` to this folder as `<Name>.md` and follow its instantiation note. `update-vault.py` lists it below; the AI never hand-edits this index.

## Provenance

Each entry carries `As of:` / `Recorded:` provenance in its body, and changing facts are superseded rather than overwritten (see Memory.md). Related entries are referenced with plain-path `Related:` lines (for example `Related: Memory/Entities/People/Jane Doe.md`).
