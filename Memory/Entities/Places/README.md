## 💡 What This Folder Is For

The registry of places of **personal significance** to the owner: home, hometown, and the locations they frequent or that carry meaning.

---

## ⚖️ What belongs here

- **Personal significance only.** A place earns a file because the owner has a relationship to it (they live there, grew up there, return to it, or hold it meaningful), not because it is well known. General facts about a city or landmark are `Library/` reference, not an entry here.
- **Personal, not venture.** A business location tied only to one of the owner's ventures is that venture's context in `Ventures/`; a place becomes a personal entry when its significance is personal.
- **Earns a file when real or recurring.** A place named once in passing stays a `Memory/Log.md` line until it proves meaningful or recurring.

---

## 🧾 Each entry

One file per place, named `<Name>.md`, holding its significance to the owner, an overview of what and where it is, the details worth keeping, and the owner's history with it. A precise address is surfaced only when the work needs it (`Profile/README.md`, Rules).

---

## ✍️ Writing an entry

`Memory/.template/Place.md` is copied into this folder as `<Name>.md` and its instantiation note followed. `update-vault.py` lists it below; this index is never edited by hand.

---

## 🕰️ Provenance and superseding

Each entry carries `As of:` and `Recorded:` provenance in its body, and a fact that changes is superseded rather than overwritten (`Memory/README.md`). Related entries are referenced with plain-path `Related:` lines (for example `Related: Memory/Entities/People/Jane Doe.md`).
