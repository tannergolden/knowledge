# 🏢 Organizations

The registry of organizations in the user's **personal life**: their gym, clinic, school, club, place of worship, or a company they are a customer of. One file per organization, holding what it is to the user, the key people there, and the history of the relationship.

## Scope

- **Personal, not ventures.** The user's own businesses live in `Ventures/`, never here. This collection is for organizations the user *relates to* from the outside - as a member, patient, student, congregant, or customer.
- **Business-only organizations belong elsewhere.** A vendor or partner organization that exists only in the user's business dealings is `Ventures/` context, not a personal Entities entry.
- **Earns a file when real or recurring.** A one-off mention stays a `Log.md` line until the relationship proves durable.

## How entries are made

Create through the engine: `memory add organization "Name"`. It stamps the frontmatter, applies the Organization template, and registers the entry below. The AI never hand-edits this index.

## Provenance

Each entry carries `As of:` / `Recorded:` provenance in its body, and changing facts are superseded rather than overwritten (see Memory.md). Key people and related entries are referenced with plain-path `Related:` lines (for example `Related: Memory/Entities/People/Jane Doe.md`).
