---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

The registry of organizations in the owner's **personal life**: their gym, clinic, school, club, place of worship, or a company they are a customer of.

---

## ⚖️ What belongs here

- **Personal, not ventures.** The owner's own businesses live in `Ventures/`, never here. This collection is for organizations the owner *relates to* from the outside: as a member, patient, student, congregant, or customer.
- **Business-only organizations belong elsewhere.** A vendor or partner organization that exists only in the owner's business dealings is its venture's context in `Ventures/`, not a personal entry.
- **Earns a file when real or recurring.** A one-off mention stays a `Memory/Log.md` line until the relationship proves durable.

---

## 🧾 Each entry

One file per organization, named `<Name>.md`, holding what it is to the owner and the owner's role there, an overview of the organization, the key people there, the history of the relationship, and its open threads.

---

## ✍️ Writing an entry

`Memory/.template/Organization.md` is copied into this folder as `<Name>.md`, and the note at its top followed. `update-vault.py` lists it below; this index is never edited by hand.

---

## 🕰️ Provenance and superseding

Each entry carries `As of:` and `Recorded:` provenance in its body, and a fact that changes is superseded rather than overwritten (`Memory/README.md`). Key people and related entries are referenced with plain-path `Related:` lines (for example `Related: Memory/Entities/People/Jane Doe.md`).
