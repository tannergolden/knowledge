## 💡 What This Folder Is For

`Inbox/` is where anything waiting to be filed lands: a document, a PDF, a photo, an export, a note
written in a hurry. The AI checks it at the start of every session and proposes a home and a clean
name for each file. Three sub-homes here are held rather than sorted: `Reports/` and `Visuals/` keep
the finished output of the research and visualize skills, and `Ideas/` keeps the discovery skill's
idea pool.

---

## 🚀 How To Use It

- **Drop files at the root of `Inbox/`**, in any format, organised or not.
- **Confirm each move.** At the next session the AI proposes where each file goes and what to call
  it, and moves nothing until you say yes. A batch of more than five comes as one table.
- **PDFs get a cleanup offer first**, to strip shadow-library watermarks and links; the originals
  are kept in `Inbox/Backups/`.
- **Nothing is deleted without your yes.** A file with no clear home stays here until you decide.

The AI reads this folder's note, [`Inbox.md`](Inbox.md), before it works here: its
rules come first, then how the folder works.
