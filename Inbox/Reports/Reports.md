# 📑 Reports

The held library for finished reports. The Research team files its research reports here and the Discovery team files its discovery reports here; this is where both kinds are kept. Reports are deliverables, produced by the standing teams and held for reference.

## Held, not sorted

Reports live here and stay here. They are not sorted into the BRAIN's permanent layers (the Library, a venture, a project): a report is a working artifact, produced, held, used, and eventually cleared, not durable reference knowledge. The session-start Inbox sweep skips this folder the way it skips the team homes, so a report is never proposed for re-sorting. Reports stay fully linted and cataloged like any other vault file.

## Good for three months, then verify

Every report is good for up to three months from its date. After three months it is stale: before it is used again, the AI must verify it, re-checking that its findings still hold, rather than trusting it as-is. The window ends on the report's `review_by` date in frontmatter, three months after it was filed, so the AI can detect a stale report by that date alone. This one flat rule replaces any per-topic freshness window: all reports, research and discovery alike, expire at three months and are re-verified on demand. A still-valid report confirmed on reuse has its `review_by` moved to three months after the verification.

## This file is the report index

`Reports.md` is the one hub that links every held report. The index below carries a wikilink to each report in this folder, so the whole library is browsable from here. This index is the **only** place a report is linked: no other file in the BRAIN cites or links to a report, which keeps the wider wikilink graph free of disposable nodes. A report can also be found by searching this folder.

The index is kept in lockstep with the folder. When a report is filed, refreshed, or cleared, its entry below is added, updated, or removed in the same edit, so the index always mirrors what is actually here. A finding that must outlive its report is still promoted into the durable layer in its own right: a `Library/` doc, a folder namesake, `AGENTS.md` or a folder's Rules, or a skill's `references/`.
