# 📑 REPORTS

<a name="top"></a>

**The held library of finished reports, each good for three months.**

_Trusted for three months, verified after that._

</div>

---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

The held library for finished reports. Research and discovery reports land here, such as the `research-and-development` skill's, whether for a company the owner runs or for the owner's own questions, and both kinds are kept here by the rules below. Reports are deliverables, held for reference.

---

## 📌 Held, not sorted

Reports live here and stay here. They are not sorted into the BRAIN's permanent layers (the Library, a venture, a project): a report is a working artifact, produced, held, used, and eventually cleared, not durable reference knowledge. The session-start sort skips this folder, as it skips the Visuals and Ideas libraries (`Inbox/README.md`), so a report is never proposed for re-sorting, yet it stays fully linted and cataloged like any other vault file.

---

## ⏳ Good for three months, then verified

Every report is good for up to three months from its date. After three months it is stale: before it is used again, it is verified, its findings re-checked to see they still hold, rather than trusted as is. The window ends on the report's `review_by` date, three months after it was filed, so a stale report shows by that date alone. This one flat rule replaces any per-topic freshness window: all reports, research and discovery alike, expire at three months and are re-verified on demand. A still-valid report confirmed on reuse has its `review_by` moved to three months after the verification.

---

## ⚖️ What is filed here, and what goes elsewhere

A finished research or discovery report is filed here: the synthesized result, with its findings and its sources. The idea records a discovery run ranks go to the idea pool (`Inbox/Ideas/`), and a standalone visual to `Inbox/Visuals/`.

A finding that must outlive its report is promoted into the durable layer in its own right before the report is filed: a `Library/` holding when it is knowledge about the world, a venture's or a project's own files when it is theirs, or, as a change proposed on its own (the root `README.md`, Ask First), a folder's `README.md` or the root `README.md`.

---

## 🧾 Filing a report

One topic, one report: a Markdown file named for its topic (`<Topic Title>.md`), typed `record`, with its `review_by` date in its frontmatter, a field of its kind (`.docs/technical/vault/Note-Schema.md`). A report already held on the same topic is refreshed in place rather than filed twice, and its index entry is added or updated in the same edit (below).

---

## 🔗 This README is the report index

This README is the one hub that links every held report. The index below carries a wikilink to each report in this folder, with its good-through date and a one-line summary, so the whole library is browsable from here. This index is the **only** place a report is linked: no other file in the BRAIN cites or links to a report, which keeps the wider wikilink graph free of disposable nodes. A report can also be found by searching this folder.

The index is kept in lockstep with the folder. When a report is filed, refreshed, or cleared, its entry below is added, updated, or removed in the same edit, so the index always mirrors what is actually here.
