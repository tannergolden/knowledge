## 💡 What This Folder Is For

`Projects/` tracks what you build, make, learn or explore on your own: side projects, creative
work, learning goals. Each project is a folder copied from `.template/`, with an `Overview.md` for
its goal and status, a `Log.md` of progress, `Notes.md` and `Resources.md`. Work for an employer, a
client or a company of yours belongs in `Ventures/`.

---

## 🚀 How To Use It

- **Mention a new project.** The AI asks whether to create its folder, and fills it in as you
  work.
- **Check the status** in each project's `Overview.md`, or ask the AI for every active project.

The AI reads this folder's note, [`Projects.md`](Projects.md), before it works here: its
rules come first, then how the folder works.
