# 🏛️ Board - Information Guide

## What this skill does

Board is the BRAIN's venture operating system. It runs a company end to end by convening the Agent Resources (AR) Fortune 500 workforce, scaled to the venture's stage, across five modes: **found** (stand up a new venture), **strategize** (set the thesis and the OKRs), **decide** (the multi-perspective decision engine), **operate** (produce real deliverables into the venture folder), and **review** (run the operating cadence and keep the venture current). It holds one overriding standard, every angle heard, one owner accountable: every relevant function is heard, work is verified before it lands, and each call resolves to one recommendation or deliverable with one accountable owner. Two rules never bend: the CEO holds the question and the functions work in isolated parallel workers, and the skill is the sole writer (agents compose and return; the skill persists, behind the write-gate). It decides and produces and names the owner; irreversible or major calls, and any gated action (money, contracts, external comms, deletes, equity), escalate to the Founder.

## How it fits the architecture

The skill composes the standing AR corporate workforce (`metadata.team: Administration`, the executive office at `Ventures/Agent Resources (AR)/Administration/`, from which the CEO, Jonathan, chairs). It convenes the whole Fortune 500 workforce: the executive office, the staff chiefs (CTO, CISO), and the functional chiefs and their specialists, with the Head of Quality (Grace) as the independent verifier; strategy framing sits with the CEO, and the retired risk and audit seats are mechanized by the write-gate, the delegation-of-authority thresholds, `check-vault.py` and the git history every change is committed to. The AR workforce produces a venture's deliverables by composing content for the venture's department files; the board skill is the sole writer that persists them. The venture template carries the matching surfaces: the `Administration/` executive office (`Strategy.md`, `Decisions.md`, `Cadence.md`), the Finance `Financial-Model.md` and `Cap-Table.md`, and the lifecycle stage in `Overview.md`. Where `discover` generates options and `research` finds what is known, board runs the company. The skill carries no agents of its own; it references the workforce by name and vendors the agents in only at Publish.

## Design basis

Built on the federated-team architecture over the expanded AR Fortune 500 workforce, rebuilt from a deep research pass that audited the prior decision-only board skill, the AR workforce, and the Ventures structure, and researched executive deliberation, Fortune 500 structure, the function-deliverable catalog, the company lifecycle (Greiner, Adizes, the Three Horizons), the operating model (OKRs, EOS/Traction, Amazon's mechanisms), the strategy-framework toolkit, the metrics-and-KPIs glossary, venture creation (lean startup, customer discovery, incorporation), governance and org design, and the AI-operating-system guardrails (verification, human-in-the-loop on irreversible actions). The durable synthesis lives in `references/` (the modes router, the lifecycle, the founding playbook, the strategy toolkit, metrics and KPIs, the operating cadence, the write-gate, verification, specialist dispatch, the department-deliverable catalog, and the decide-mode doctrine: deliberation, lenses, frameworks, synthesis, memo, dispatch).
