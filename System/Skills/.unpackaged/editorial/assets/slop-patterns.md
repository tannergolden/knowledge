# AI-slop pattern catalog

Grouped to match `references/slop-rubric.md`. Scan a draft for these; flag each hit with `location - group - the cited span - the fix`.

## 1. Content (says nothing specific)

- Significance inflation: important, crucial, vital, essential, notable, key, significant (asserted, not shown).
- Vague attribution: experts say, studies show, research suggests, it is widely regarded, many believe (no named source).
- Promotional adjectives: rich, powerful, seamless, robust, game-changing, world-class, best-in-class, state-of-the-art.
- Superficial -ing analysis: highlighting the importance of, underscoring the need for, reflecting a broader trend, showcasing, demonstrating the value of, emphasizing the significance of.

## 2. Language (the slop vocabulary, Constitution 9.2)

- Banned words (9.2): delve, leverage (verb), utilize, robust, comprehensive, streamlined, cutting-edge, synergy, paradigm, holistic, actionable, empower, foster, harness, elevate, underscore, crucial, vital, notable, importantly, furthermore, moreover, additionally.
- Three-tier inflation, the strongest tells: leverage to use, utilize to use, robust to strong or solid.
- Copula avoidance: serves as, functions as, acts as, represents, stands as, plays a role as (usually "is").
- Synonym cycling: the tool / the solution / the platform / the offering for one thing (name it once).
- Filler: in order to (to), due to the fact that (because), at this point in time (now), a number of (some / a count), in the event that (if), with regard to (about), it is important to note that (cut).

## 3. Structure (the shape gives it away)

- Transition abuse: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, In conclusion, Importantly, To that end, That said (as connective filler between paragraphs).
- Inline-header lists: every bullet a **bold lead-in:** then a clause, where prose would read better.
- Uniform sentence rhythm: every sentence the same length and shape (vary it).
- List inflation: bulleting prose that should stay prose.
- Bold and emoji excess: bolding until nothing is emphasized; emoji as decoration.

## 4. Communication (the chatbot tells)

- Chatbot artifacts: I hope this helps, Let me know if you need anything else, I'd be happy to, Great question, Certainly, Absolutely, Of course, Feel free to.
- Hedges: it's worth noting, it's important to remember, it should be mentioned, arguably, in many ways, to some extent.
- Sycophancy: flattering the reader or the prompt before answering.
- Generic uplift conclusions: Ultimately the key is, By embracing these strategies, At the end of the day, When all is said and done.

## 5. Meta (over-working the text)

- Over-structuring short text: headers, bullets, and bold on a three-sentence answer.
- Over-polishing: sanding off every natural variation until the prose is frictionless and dead.

## The fix (the only real one)

Write as you speak and take a position. Replace the inflated word with the plain one, cut the hedge and the chatbot line, let "is" be "is", and end on the last real point. Do not swap one slop word for another: utilize to leverage fixes nothing. Shorter wins when meaning is preserved, and "leave it alone" is a valid verdict.
