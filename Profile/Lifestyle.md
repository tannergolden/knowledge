# 🌿 Lifestyle

How the owner **lives** - the stable, enduring patterns of daily life (the physical and environmental dimensions of wellness). Boundary: this is the *durable* way of living; what the owner is doing or prioritizing *right now*, and routines that change month to month, live in `Profile/Focus.md`. Tastes (favorite foods, media) are `Profile/Preferences.md`; medical conditions are `Profile/Health/`; this is behavior and pattern.

## Snapshot

A few lines on how the owner lives. Fill last.

## Daily Rhythm & Chronotype

When the owner wakes, works, peaks, and winds down; morning lark or night owl; the typical shape of a day.

## Sleep

Sleep schedule, duration, and quality patterns.

## Diet & Nutrition

The owner's eating pattern and dietary approach (cross-reference dietary restrictions in `Profile/Health/` and food *tastes* in `Profile/Preferences.md`).

## Exercise & Fitness

Movement and fitness habits - what, how often, how it fits the week.

## Substance Use

Habitual use of caffeine, alcohol, tobacco, or other substances - patterns, not one-off events. Held with discretion (`Profile/README.md`, Rules).

## Rituals & Routines

Recurring personal rituals and standing routines that define ordinary life (the stable ones; current or shifting routines are `Profile/Focus.md`).

## Living Environment

The owner's home setup, neighborhood character, and the environment they live and work in.

## Recreation & Leisure

How the owner rests, plays, and recharges (cross-reference hobbies and interests in `Profile/Preferences.md`).
