What the owner prizes and how they judge right from wrong - the facet to consult when **weighing options or giving advice**. ## Life Philosophy

How the owner views life, what they believe about people and work, and any motto or idea they live by.

## Ethical Orientation

How the owner reasons about right and wrong - their ethical framework (rule-based, outcomes-based, virtue) and the Moral Foundations (care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity, liberty) they weight most. Plus principles lived by, lines they will not cross, and non-negotiables.

## Motivation

What drives the owner (Self-Determination Theory: autonomy, competence, relatedness; intrinsic vs extrinsic). What energizes them and what drains them.

## Purpose & Meaning

What gives the owner's life meaning, what they feel called to do, and the legacy they want to leave.

## Definition of Success

What a good life or good work looks like to the owner, in their own terms.

## Role Models & Influences

People the owner admires and why, plus books, ideas, or movements that shaped them - an indirect, high-signal read on aspired-to values.

## Anti-Role-Models

People or patterns the owner actively does not want to become - what they define themselves against.

## What I Stand For

The commitments the owner actively champions.

## What I Stand Against

The things the owner actively opposes.
