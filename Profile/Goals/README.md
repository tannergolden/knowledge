---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

The owner's durable aspirations - the long-horizon "what I am working toward" that is part of who they are (the possible self they are moving toward). Boundary: this holds **stable** goals only. This week's priorities and current routines live in `Profile/Focus.md`; concrete project execution and tasks live in `Projects/`. A standing goal that would still be true in a year belongs here; the next action toward it does not.

## 🧭 Direction

The overarching arc: where the owner wants their life and work to go. The frame the individual goals serve.

---

## 🪞 Possible Selves

The selves that pull the owner forward and back: the **hoped-for self** they are moving toward, the **feared self** they want to avoid becoming, and the **ought self** they feel they should be. These drive motivated behavior more than stated goals do.
