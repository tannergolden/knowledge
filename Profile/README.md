## 💡 What This Folder Is For

`Profile/` describes you, facet by facet: `Biography.md`, `Personality.md`, `Values/`, `Goals/`,
`Skills/`, `Preferences.md`, `Relationships.md`, `Finances.md`, `Lifestyle.md`, `Health/`, `Focus.md`,
and `Working Style.md`, which tells the AI how to work with you and is read at the start of every
session. In the public template every facet is a blank scaffold; in your own vault, which stays
private, they hold the most personal notes you have.

## 🚀 How To Use It

- **Fill it with the `/identify` skill.** It interviews you, writes what you confirm, and marks
  anything it inferred until you approve it.
- **Edit by hand** for a quick correction. The AI treats these notes as read-only unless you ask it
  to change them.
- **Keep the vault private.** Every note here is tagged sensitive, and the AI surfaces it only when
  a task needs it.

The AI reads this folder's note, [`Profile.md`](Profile.md), before it works here: its
rules come first, then how the folder works.
