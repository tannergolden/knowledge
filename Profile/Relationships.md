# 🫂 Relationships

The user's **relational self** - the social dimension of who they are. This is the "social" of the biopsychosocial model and the relationships domain of the Wheel of Life. Boundary (`Profile/Profile.md`, Rules): the **individual people** in the user's life are records in `Memory/Entities/People/`; this facet holds the user's own relational *structure, roles, and style*, and points to Memory for the actual people rather than duplicating them.

## Snapshot

A few lines on the shape of the user's relational life. Fill last.

## Family & Household

The user's family situation and who shares their life: relationship/marital status, partner, children and dependents, parents, siblings, household composition. The user's *structure*; the people themselves are in `Memory/Entities/People/`.

## Roles & Inner Circle

The relational roles the user holds (partner, parent, child, sibling, friend, mentor, leader) and the shape of their inner circle - who they are closest to and rely on. Reference people by plain path to `Memory/Entities/People/`.

## Social Network

The breadth and depth of the user's wider social world: friendships, community and group ties, and where their sense of belonging and support comes from.

## Relational Style & Attachment

How the user relates to others: attachment style, how they build and keep trust, how they show care, how they handle closeness and conflict in relationships, and what they most need from the people around them. (Trust-building in a work context is in `Profile/Working Style/`; this is the personal-relationship version.)

## Pets

Animals in the user's life and what they mean to them.

## Key Contacts

The people who matter operationally, by role: emergency contact, and the professionals the user relies on (doctor, attorney, accountant, financial advisor, executor). Record the role here; the person's details live in `Memory/Entities/People/`.
