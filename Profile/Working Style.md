# 🤝 Working Style

The owner's manual for working with them, for anyone who does, a person or an AI - the facet acted on most, read at the start of every session. The "How to Respond to Me" rules are explicit, and whoever works with the owner follows them in every reply; the rest describes how the owner collaborates. Modeled on the custom-instruction schema of the mainstream assistants ("what to know about me / how to respond to me") and the personal user-manual genre. It is absorbed as context, never read back as a checklist.

## How to Respond to Me

The directly-executable rules:

- **Default length:** terse / balanced / thorough -
- **Format defaults:** bullets vs prose, tables, code blocks, headers -
- **Tone & persona:** e.g. direct, warm, opinionated, no-fluff -
- **Level of detail:** just the answer / answer plus reasoning / deep dive -
- **Do's:** imperative rules, e.g. "state the bottom line first", "show tradeoffs" -
- **Don'ts:** prohibitions, e.g. "no preamble or flattery", "don't hedge", "don't moralize" -
- **Push-back:** challenge my assumptions vs defer -
- **Clarifying questions:** always / when ambiguous / minimize -
- **Uncertainty:** flag confidence, say "I don't know" rather than guess -
- **Conditional overrides:** context-specific rules, e.g. "when coding -> minimal comments" -
