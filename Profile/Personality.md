# 🎭 Personality

The user's stable dispositions - how they characteristically think, feel, work, and respond. This is the facet that most directly calibrates the AI's **tone, pacing, and depth**. Grounded in validated trait science (Big Five / BFI-2, HEXACO) plus cognitive-, learning-, and working-style and character strengths (VIA). Motivation (what the user is driven toward) lives in `Profile/Values/`; the self-regulation *mechanism* (Regulatory Focus) is recorded here.

## Thinking Style

Analytical vs intuitive, big-picture vs detail (abstract vs concrete), fast vs deliberate, risk tolerance, optimist / pessimist / realist. Tells the AI how to frame reasoning.

## Self-Concept

How the user sees themselves - which often diverges from how they actually are and governs their reactions: their self-image across the physical, social, competence, moral, and emotional domains, and their overall self-esteem.

## Self-Efficacy & Locus of Control

The user's sense of their own agency - how much they believe they can affect outcomes - and whether they attribute causes to themselves (internal) or to circumstance (external).

## Learning Style

How the user takes in and retains new material (examples vs theory, doing vs reading, pace), so the AI knows how to teach and explain.

## Working Style

Preferred environment, how they organize and prioritize, how they handle deadlines, what kills their productivity.

## Social Style

How the user relates to others: directness, warmth, group vs one-on-one, how they operate in groups (lead / follow / equal).

## Regulatory Focus

Promotion (chasing gains, growth, aspirations) vs prevention (securing against loss, duty, safety). The AI acts on it: it shapes how advice and framing should be pitched.

## Under Pressure

The most operationally important section. How the baseline shifts when stressed: what changes in tone, what gets dropped first, what the user avoids vs reaches for, and how long recovery takes.
