# 💰 Finances

The user's **personal financial self** - their stable financial picture and how they relate to money. The financial dimension of wellness. Boundary: a *business's* finances live in `Ventures/`; dated transactions and bills live in `Memory/`; this holds the durable personal picture. **Sensitive (Constitution 4.4), and secrets are pointers, not values:** record that an account, document, or policy *exists and where it lives*, never the account number, SSN, password, or balance to the dollar. Surface only when directly relevant.

## Snapshot

A few lines on the user's overall financial situation. Fill last.

## Financial Situation

Income level and sources, overall financial security or stress, and economic stability (employment security, housing and food security). The shape, not the cents.

## Assets

A roster by type - bank and brokerage accounts, retirement (401k / IRA / pension), real estate, vehicles, business interests, other valuables - named by institution or kind, not by account number.

## Liabilities

Debts and obligations by type - mortgage, student or auto loans, credit cards, personal debts - their kind and rough scale, not exact figures.

## Net Worth

A rough overall picture, updated on change.

## Insurance

Policies the user holds (life, health, disability, long-term care, home, auto) by carrier and type, with the policy location, not the number.

## Money Management Style

How the user handles money day to day: spending and saving habits, budgeting approach, and risk tolerance. Their money *values and orientation* live in `Profile/Values/`; this is the behavior.

## Financial Goals & Constraints

Standing financial aims and hard limits (cross-reference the stable goals in `Profile/Goals/`).

## Legal & Estate

The existence and location of estate and legal instruments - will, trust, power of attorney, beneficiaries, advance directives (cross-reference `Profile/Health/`) - and the named executor. Record that they exist and where, never their contents. Funeral or final wishes, if the user records them, go here too.

## Subscriptions & Recurring

Notable recurring commitments worth remembering.
