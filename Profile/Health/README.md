---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

Stable health attributes and accessibility needs, kept only insofar as they change **how to help**. This is for awareness, **not diagnostic use**, and is surfaced only when directly relevant to the task (`Profile/README.md`, Rules). It is not a medical record. Each specific condition is isolated in its own entry so that a benign query never drags in a diagnosis; this page holds only the always-useful summary.

---

## 📸 Snapshot

A terse, general line on health status, only if it bears on how to help. Fill last.

---

## 🤝 Implications for How to Help

The part that gets used: how to pace, format, or adapt help, and what to avoid. Keep it minimal and considerate.

---

## 📋 Medical History

The durable medical background to be aware of: allergies, significant past conditions and surgeries, current medications and supplements, immunizations, and relevant family medical history. Terse, for awareness, not a clinical record. Sensitive.

---

## 🛡️ Insurance & Advance Directives

Health insurance (carrier and coverage, by reference) and any advance directives - living will, healthcare proxy, DNR - their existence and location only (estate-side documents live in `Profile/Finances.md`). Pointers, never contents.

---

One terse entry per condition or accessibility need, isolated for privacy. Add one by copying `Profile/.template/Health Condition.md` here as `<Title>.md`; `update-vault.py` keeps the list below.
