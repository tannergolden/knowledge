# 🎨 Preferences

The user's interests and tastes - what they like, consume, use, and dislike. These are topical and personalize *suggestions* (what to recommend, reference, or avoid). Boundary: how the user wants the AI to *behave* is `Profile/Working Style/`; what the user is skilled at is `Profile/Skills/`. This is about taste, not instruction or competence.
