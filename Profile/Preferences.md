# 🎨 Preferences

The owner's interests and tastes - what they like, consume, use, and dislike. These are topical and personalize *suggestions* (what to recommend, reference, or avoid). Boundary: how the owner likes to be *worked with*, by a person or an AI, is `Profile/Working Style.md`; what the owner is skilled at is `Profile/Skills/`. This is about taste, not working style or competence.
