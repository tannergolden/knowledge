## 💡 Purpose

Myths, cosmologies and legends as belief: the gods and stories of a culture's sacred world, folk belief and superstition, and comparative mythology, along with the myths of traditions no longer practiced, such as the Greek and the Norse. A living tradition's sacred story goes to that tradition's division; folktales and legends as verbal art to Language & Literature / Oral & Folk Literature; cosmology as science to Science & Mathematics / Astronomy & Space.
