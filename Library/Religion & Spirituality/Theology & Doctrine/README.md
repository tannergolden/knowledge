## Purpose

Theology and doctrine in general or across traditions: systematic and comparative theology, creeds and dogma, and the methods of scriptural interpretation. The theology of one tradition goes to that tradition's division, such as Religion & Spirituality / Abrahamic Traditions; the philosophy of religion, argued without a tradition, to Philosophy & Mind.
