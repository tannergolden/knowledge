# 🕊️ Library / Religion & Spirituality /

## Purpose
Religion and spirituality studied neutrally, with every tradition treated as a peer and none privileged. It gathers comparative study, theology, mythology, ritual, and secular or contemplative practice. The social science of religious communities goes to Society; the history of a faith or sacred site goes to History.

## Class here / not here
- Class here: Any tradition studied neutrally, as a peer
- Class here: Theology, doctrine, ritual, and contemplative or secular spirituality
- Class here: Sacred myth and cosmology as belief
- Not here: Abstract ethics or metaphysics argued without a tradition - goes to Philosophy & Mind
- Not here: The sociology, demography, or politics of religious groups - goes to Society & Social Sciences
- Not here: Folktales and proverbs as verbal art - goes to Language & Literature / Oral & Folk Literature
