# 📁 Library / Religion & Spirituality / Comparative Religion /

## Purpose

Religion studied across traditions: the comparison of religions, the methods of religious studies, interfaith relations, and the traditions with no division of their own, such as Zoroastrianism, new religious movements and the religions of the ancient world. One tradition goes to its own division, such as Religion & Spirituality / Abrahamic Traditions; the sociology of religion to Society & Social Sciences / Sociology; the philosophy of religion, argued without a tradition, to Philosophy & Mind.
