## 💡 Purpose

The religious systems of indigenous, tribal and local peoples worldwide: animism, shamanism, ancestral and nature spirits, and local folk religion, each studied as a peer. Myth and folk belief compared across cultures go to Religion & Spirituality / Mythology & Folklore; a people's wider culture to Society & Social Sciences / Anthropology.
