## 💡 Purpose

Life and living things: cells, molecular biology and biochemistry, genetics, evolution, organisms from microbes to plants and animals, ecology, human anatomy and physiology, neuroscience, and paleontology. Human health and its treatment go to Technology & Applied Science / Medicine & Health Sciences; farming and animal husbandry to Technology & Applied Science / Agriculture & Food Science; ecosystems as environmental systems to Science & Mathematics / Environmental Science; human evolution to Society & Social Sciences / Anthropology.
