# 🔬 Library / Science & Mathematics /

## Purpose
The formal and natural sciences and how scientific knowledge is made. Applied science such as engineering and medicine goes to Technology; the theory of computation and data management go to Information & Computing.

| Division (Layer 3) | Scope |

| Statistics & Probability | Probability theory and statistical inference |

| Chemistry | Substances, reactions, and materials at the molecular scale |
| Biology & Life Sciences | Life, organisms, genetics, ecology, evolution, paleontology |
| Earth Sciences | Geology, oceanography, meteorology, the earth system |
| Astronomy & Space | Astronomy, astrophysics, cosmology, and space science |
| Environmental Science | Climate, ecosystems, and environmental systems as natural science |
| Philosophy & Method of Science | Scientific method, and the philosophy and history of science |
