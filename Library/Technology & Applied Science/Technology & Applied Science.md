# ⚙️ Library / Technology & Applied Science /

## Purpose
Knowledge applied to practical ends: the making of working things and the running of operations. Pure science goes to Science & Mathematics; computer science as a field of study and information science go to Information & Computing.

## Divisions
| Division (Layer 3) | Scope |
|--------------------|-------|
| Engineering | Mechanical, electrical, civil, chemical, and other engineering |
| Medicine & Health Sciences | Clinical medicine, public health, nursing, psychiatry |
| Applied Computing & Software | Building and deploying software, systems, applied machine learning, and cybersecurity |
| Agriculture & Food Science | Farming, food production, husbandry, veterinary medicine, and food technology |
| Energy & Materials | Energy systems, and materials science and engineering |
| Manufacturing & Industry | Production, industrial processes, and quality engineering |
| Transportation | Vehicles, logistics, and transport systems |
| Operations & Management | Applied operations, project, and quality management practice |
| Infrastructure & Built Environment | Construction, building engineering, urban planning, utilities |

- Not here: Computer science as a field of study - goes to Information & Computing / Computing & Computer Science
- Not here: Economic or organizational theory - goes to Society & Social Sciences
