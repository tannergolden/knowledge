## 💡 Purpose

Energy and materials as technology: energy systems and power generation, renewable, nuclear and fossil energy, storage and batteries, and materials science and engineering, from metallurgy to polymers. The physics of energy goes to Science & Mathematics / Physics; the chemistry of materials to Science & Mathematics / Chemistry; the grids and utilities that serve a place to Technology & Applied Science / Infrastructure & Built Environment.
