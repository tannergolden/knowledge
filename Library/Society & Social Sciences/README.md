# 👥 Library / Society & Social Sciences /

## Purpose

How human societies are structured, governed, and sustained: sociology and anthropology, politics and law, economics and commerce, education and media, social policy, and military and security studies.

| Division (Layer 3) | Scope |

| Anthropology | Cultural and social anthropology, ethnography, human origins |
| Politics & Government | Political science, governance, ideology, international relations |
| Law & Justice | Legal systems, jurisprudence, rights, and justice |
| Economics | Economic theory, markets, and finance and policy as a discipline |
| Education | Education theory, policy, and systems |
