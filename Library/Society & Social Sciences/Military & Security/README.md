## 💡 Purpose

War, defense and security as a discipline: military science and strategy, armed forces, intelligence, security and peace studies, and cyber defense. A specific war's history goes to History & Geography / World History or Regional & Area Histories; weapons engineering to Technology & Applied Science / Engineering; diplomacy to Society & Social Sciences / Politics & Government.
