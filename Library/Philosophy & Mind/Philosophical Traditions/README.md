## 💡 Purpose

Philosophy by school, period and thinker, Eastern and Western, read as philosophy: its history, from ancient philosophy and Stoicism to rationalism and existentialism. Thought argued within a religious tradition, such as Buddhist or Confucian teaching, goes to that tradition's division in Religion & Spirituality; one question pursued across schools to its own division, such as Philosophy & Mind / Ethics & Moral Philosophy.
