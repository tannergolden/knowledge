# 🗂️ Library / Information & Computing /

## Purpose
How knowledge and information are organized, stored, computed, and transmitted, plus the general reference works that span every subject. This class holds the discipline of information itself; the user's own data lives in Profile or Memory, and building working software belongs to Technology.

## Divisions
| Division (Layer 3) | Scope |
|--------------------|-------|
| Information Science | Theory of information, its measurement, storage, retrieval, and flow |
| Knowledge Organization | Classification, cataloging, taxonomies, ontologies, metadata |
| Computing & Computer Science | Algorithms, computation theory, programming languages, data structures as a field of study |
| Data & Information Management | Databases, datasets, data management, analytics practice, data literacy |
| Internet & Web | The internet, the web, networks, protocols, online platforms |
| Publishing & Broadcasting | Publishing, the press, and broadcasting institutions and infrastructure |
| Libraries & Archives | Librarianship, archives, records management, documentation |
| General Reference | Encyclopedias, dictionaries, almanacs, cross-subject general works |
| Systems & Cybernetics | Systems theory, cybernetics, information and complexity across domains |

## Class here / not here
- Class here: The study of information, computing, and how knowledge is organized
- Class here: General cross-subject reference works (encyclopedias, dictionaries)
- Class here: Cataloging and classification practice, this Library's own discipline
- Not here: Building or deploying working software - goes to Technology & Applied Science / Applied Computing & Software
- Not here: The mathematics of statistics and probability - goes to Science & Mathematics / Statistics & Probability
- Not here: The social science of media and journalism - goes to Society & Social Sciences / Media & Communication
