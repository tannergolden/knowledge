---

## 💡 What This Folder Is For

`Library/` holds reference knowledge, organised by discipline into ten classes the way a real
library is, so the AI can draw on it to answer and reason. It holds nothing personal: who you are
lives in `Profile/`, what happened in `Memory/`, and your companies in `Ventures/`. Every holding is
tagged by how long it stays true, and nothing with less than five years of life is filed here.

---

## 🚀 How To Use It

- **Add a source through `Inbox/`.** The AI classifies it, checks its freshness and files it in
  the right class and division.
- **Ask a question.** The AI searches the Library before reaching for the web, and cites what it
  used.
- **Offline reference works**, such as Wikipedia, can be switched on under
  `Reference & Encyclopedia/` with `.github/scripts/knowledge-base.py`; every source ships off.

The AI reads this folder's note, [`Library.md`](Library.md), before it works here: its
rules come first, then how the folder works.
