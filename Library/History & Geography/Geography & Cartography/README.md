## 💡 Purpose

The earth as places and regions: physical geography, the surface of the earth region by region; human geography, how people settle and use the land; and maps, atlases and map-making. The processes that shape the earth go to Science & Mathematics / Earth Sciences; journeys and accounts of places to History & Geography / Travel & Exploration; urban planning to Technology & Applied Science / Infrastructure & Built Environment.
