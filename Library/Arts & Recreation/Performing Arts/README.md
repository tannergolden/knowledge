## 💡 Purpose

The arts of live performance: theatre and acting, dance and choreography, opera and musical theatre as staged works, stagecraft, and circus and other live forms, with their history. A play read as a text goes to Language & Literature / Drama; the music of an opera to Arts & Recreation / Music; performance made for the screen to Arts & Recreation / Film & Media Arts.
