## 💡 Purpose

Craft and the decorative and applied arts: ceramics, textiles, woodwork, glass, metalwork and jewelry, as art forms with their traditions and history. Learning a craft as a pastime goes to Life & Practice / Hobbies & Skills; objects designed for industry to Arts & Recreation / Design; industrial production to Technology & Applied Science / Manufacturing & Industry.
