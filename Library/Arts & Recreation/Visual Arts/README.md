# 📁 Library / Arts & Recreation / Visual Arts /

## Purpose

The visual arts by form: painting, sculpture, drawing, printmaking, photography and newer visual media, with their movements, artists, history and criticism. Film and screen media go to Arts & Recreation / Film & Media Arts; craft and the decorative arts to Arts & Recreation / Crafts & Applied Arts; the philosophy of art in general to Philosophy & Mind / Aesthetics.
