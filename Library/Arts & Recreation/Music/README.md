## 💡 Purpose

Music of every tradition and genre: its composition, performance, theory and instruments, its composers and performers, and its history. Opera and musical theatre as staged works go to Arts & Recreation / Performing Arts; lyrics read as poetry to Language & Literature / Poetry; learning an instrument as a pastime to Life & Practice / Hobbies & Skills.
