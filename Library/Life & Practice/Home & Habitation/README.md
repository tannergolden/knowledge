## 💡 Purpose

Running a home: household management and organization, cleaning, home repair and maintenance, gardening, and pet care. Building and construction go to Technology & Applied Science / Infrastructure & Built Environment; farming to Technology & Applied Science / Agriculture & Food Science; interior design to Arts & Recreation / Design; a move or a renovation the owner plans to `Projects/`.
