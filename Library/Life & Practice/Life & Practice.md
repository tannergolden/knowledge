# 🧰 Library / Life & Practice /

## Purpose
General practical knowledge for the business of living, written for anyone. It is general reference (how budgeting works), never the user's own data, which lives in Profile or Memory. The academic theory behind a practice goes to its science class.

## Class here / not here
- Class here: How-to and practical reference for living, written for anyone
- Class here: General method (budgeting, a balanced diet), never the user's own data
- Class here: Personal-interest skill-building and hobbies
- Not here: The user's actual finances, health records, or relationships - goes to Profile/ or Memory/
- Not here: The academic science behind a practice (metabolism, economics) - goes to Science & Mathematics or Society & Social Sciences
- Not here: An activity as an art form or sport culture - goes to Arts & Recreation
