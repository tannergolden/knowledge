## 💡 Purpose

Pastimes and skills learned for personal interest, as how-to: crafting, collecting, learning an instrument, amateur photography and other hobbies. An art or a sport as practice and culture goes to its Arts & Recreation division; the owner's own skills to `Profile/Skills/`.

This README adds no rules of its own.
