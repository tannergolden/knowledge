# 📁 Library / Language & Literature / Literary Criticism /

## Purpose

The study of literature across works: literary theory and criticism, literary history, genres and movements, and comparative literature. A single work or author, and the criticism of it, goes to the division for its form, such as Language & Literature / Drama for a study of one play; the philosophy of art in general to Philosophy & Mind / Aesthetics; the criticism of another art to its Arts & Recreation division.
