## Purpose

Prose literature: novels and short stories of every genre, historical fiction included, essays and literary nonfiction, their writers, and the criticism of one work or writer. Biography and memoir go to History & Geography / Biography & Memoir; folktales to Language & Literature / Oral & Folk Literature; the craft of writing fiction to Language & Literature / Writing & Rhetoric.
